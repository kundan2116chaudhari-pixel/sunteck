FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Sunteck Naigaon

Got questions? Find clear answers regarding pricing, location, RERA, site visits, and amenities.

1. Where is Sunteck One World located?

Sunteck One World is located in Naigaon East, Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), offering convenient connectivity to Mumbai, Thane, Vasai, and Virar through the Western Railway corridor and major road networks.

2. What configurations are available at Sunteck One World?

The project offers thoughtfully designed 1 BHK Platinum (407 sq.ft) and 2 BHK Grand (550 sq.ft) luxury residences. Please contact our sales team for detailed floor plans and current tower inventory availability.

3. What is the starting price of apartments?

Starting prices begin from ₹37.9 Lacs* Onwards for 1 BHK Platinum and ₹49.9 Lacs* Onwards for 2 BHK Grand residences. Contact our sales team to receive the latest price list, ongoing festival offers, and flexible payment plans.

4. Is Sunteck One World a RERA-registered project?

Yes. Sunteck Naigaon is an official MahaRERA-registered township. Official RERA Registration Numbers include P99000032834, P99000033099, P99000033218, and P99000033157.

5. What amenities are available at Sunteck One World?

Residents can enjoy 55+ world-class lifestyle amenities including a Grand Clubhouse & Lounge, Swimming Pool, State-of-the-Art Gym, Box Cricket Turf, Children's Adventure Park, Indoor Games Arena, Private Mini Preview Theatre, and Elevated Podium Gardens.

6. Can I schedule a guided site visit?

Yes! You can schedule a guided site visit at your preferred date and time. We offer complimentary AC cab pick-and-drop assistance from key suburban locations. Simply submit your details through the callback form or contact our sales team.

7. Can I download the brochure and floor plans?

Yes. You can instantly download the official 11MB PDF brochure and detailed floor plans by clicking the 'Download Brochure' button on this page or filling out the quick enquiry form.

8. Is Sunteck One World suitable for investment?

Absolutely. As the largest 150-acre integrated township in Mumbai's Western Suburbs, Sunteck Naigaon offers strong rental yields, rapid infrastructure growth (Metro Line 10 & Bhayandar Sea Link), and high long-term capital appreciation.

9. Are home loans available?

Yes. Major leading banks and financial institutions (such as SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Bank of Baroda) offer pre-approved home loan facilities, subject to borrower eligibility.

10. How can I contact the sales team?

You can call us directly at +91 93218 15517, send a WhatsApp message, or fill out the enquiry form on this page. Our sales team will get in touch immediately to assist you.
